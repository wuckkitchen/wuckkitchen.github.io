Unknot — Privacy Policy

Wuck Kitchen · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who we are

Unknot is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Wuck Kitchen (developer: Lara Karaahmet). Contact: wuck@wuck.app

What the game stores on your device

Everything the game remembers stays on your device, in the game's own storage:

  • your best score and a few totals (games played, puzzles solved);
  • your settings (sound, reminders);
  • an unfinished game (score, current puzzle, where the dots are and the time left), so you can continue after closing the app. It is removed when that game ends.

None of it is sent to us. There is no account, and we use no analytics or other tracking service. Like other app data, it can be part of your iPhone's own backups. Deleting the game deletes it. The game can be played offline; ads need an internet connection.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Unknot is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • a banner at the bottom of the screen, also while you play (it never covers the puzzle);
  • now and then a full-screen ad after a finished game, never during play;
  • optional ads you choose to watch: for extra time when the clock runs out, or to continue an unfinished game when you open the app again.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

In these regions Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can play either way, and you can change your choice later from the game's main menu under "Privacy choices" (shown where these rules apply).

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

Unknot may ask for permission to track you with Apple's prompt. If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads and to measure ads. If you don't, ads are still shown but the identifier is not used. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Reminders (optional)

Unknot can remind you to play with notifications. They are off until you turn them on, and iOS asks for your permission first. The reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself; nothing about them is sent to us or to anyone else. Turn them off any time with the bell on the main menu, or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Unknot.

Children

Unknot is not specifically directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy. Questions and requests: wuck@wuck.app

Changes

When we update this policy we change the date at the top of this page.
