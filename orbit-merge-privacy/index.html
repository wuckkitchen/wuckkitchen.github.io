Orbit Merge — Privacy Policy

Wuck Kitchen · Last updated: October 22, 2026

Who we are

Orbit Merge is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Wuck Kitchen (developer: Lara Karaahmet). Contact: wuck@wuck.app

What the game stores on your device

Your progress stays on your device, in the game's own storage: best scores, statistics, achievements, discovered balls, daily-mission results, settings and a saved game you can continue.

None of it is sent to us. There is no account, and we use no analytics or other tracking service. Deleting the game deletes this data. The game can be played offline; ads need an internet connection.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Orbit Merge is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • a banner at the bottom of the menu and results screens, never during play;
  • after a game ends, an optional short ad you can choose to watch to continue once;
  • between games, a full-screen ad at most once every few minutes, never during play.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

In these regions Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can play either way, and you can change your choice later in the game under Settings → "Ad privacy choices".

Tracking

Orbit Merge does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency) and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Children

Orbit Merge is not specifically directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy. Questions and requests: wuck@wuck.app

Changes

When we update this policy we change the date at the top of this page.
